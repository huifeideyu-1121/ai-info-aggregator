### [I built a free iPhone app that tracks what a car actually costs to run, not just the fuel](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/1wj9h9g/i_built_a_free_iphone_app_that_tracks_what_a_car/)

## AI新技术/新模型

### [I built a side project to test TypeSafe's Jev model with no generative LLM that scores your project idea](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/1wiw6tk/i_built_a_side_project_to_test_typesafes_jev/)
- **来源**：Reddit r/SideProject
- **评分**：5/10
- **标签**：`#Jev模型` `#无生成式LLM` `#开源side project`
- **摘要**：基于 TypeSafe 的 Jev 模型构建了开源侧项目 killmyidea.stemonte.io，输入项目想法后通过约 10 个问题与分类并行评分，仅输出数值化结果而不生成对话文本。相比此前需 LLM 耗时 30-60 秒进行多轮推理评估，Jev 并行处理所有请求直接返回分数，且因输入兼容性广而适用场景近乎无限。
